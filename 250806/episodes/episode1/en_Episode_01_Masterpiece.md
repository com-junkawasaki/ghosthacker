### Ghost Hacker Episode 01: Fear of God (Masterpiece Version)

#### **Act 1: Everyday Life and Anomaly**

The canal in Shibuya is quiet today. My office is located along this canal. Behind the large floor-to-ceiling windows stretches a vast expanse that seems to be a slice of the Okinawan sea - deep, clear, and blue water. Occasionally, a water bus passes silently, gliding like a ripple on the surface. While gazing at this scenery, I was thinking about my job.

"Ghost Hacker." That's my profession.

A life form that exists without a physical body, composed purely of information - we call them "Ghosts." They can be strong emotions of people, souls that have become information after death, or pure digital intelligences born as AIs. Most ghosts are harmless, but some adversely affect people's spirits (spirits). Our job as ghost hackers is to find these lost ghosts, unravel the knots in their hearts, and return them to the Tree of Life, the source of life.

"Heart knots exist in everyone." I think. "Whether alive or dead."

In the depths of the office, Kaede was submerged in the sea of data, surrounded by walls of monitors as usual. As her fingers danced across the keyboard, complex spectrographs and cascading green code appeared on the screens. She is our team's digital anchor. She constantly monitors information space security and protects us from illegal hackers and "malignant ghosts" - dangerous entities that cause spiritual pollution. Right now, she seems to be investigating the cases of spiritual pollution that have been reported more frequently recently - deep wrinkles are etched between her brows.

"Be careful, Tamaki." She says without taking her eyes off the screen. "The information flow around here is starting to get cloudy. There might be an illegal ghost hacker nearby."

"I know." I nod.

Next to her, Hibiki was sitting with headphones on, eyes closed, concentrating on something. The air around him alone feels different. The ambient music he creates is not just BGM - it's a "musical prescription" to stabilize our spirits and adjust spatial frequencies. Our work always stands on this delicate balance. Without him, I probably couldn't safely dive into a ghost's heart.

That afternoon seemed no different from usual. What broke the silence was not a physical sound.

A semi-transparent window suddenly appeared in the office space. It was a holographic call indicator, rippling like a droplet falling on water, but certainly disturbing the space. Only the parsed ghost ID filled with pre-analysis noise was displayed on the window.

"A client." I say, and Kaede traces her console with her fingertips, safely pulling the call into our security network. One of her monitors shows a preview of an unstable human figure.

I raise my right hand in space without standing up. Then, I lightly draw a circle with my index finger and thumb. It's our hand sign to respond to a call.

In that instant, the office scenery shimmers like a heat haze, and my consciousness accesses another virtual space. It's a consulting room designed solely for safe communication with ghosts, completely shielded from external interference. In the center of the minimalist space with white as the base color stood the human figure from earlier.

A "ghost" in the form of a young man dressed in a black suit. His translucent body flickers unreliably, his light fluctuating violently like a candle in a stormy night. His mental instability felt even more vivid here than in the physical office.

My shoulder fairy Nee-Chan suddenly wakes up. She is my special partner. Normally she appears as a fairy the size of a palm, but her essence is pure information. The moment she saw the young ghost, her body burst open, scattering like stained glass shards into countless fragments of light in the office.

The information she reads flows into my brain. It's not just a color of emotion. More specific, and accompanied by pain - keywords.

'Confession' 'Betrayal' 'God's Silence' 'Unforgivable Sin'――.

By the time the light particles gathered again and Nee-Chan returned to my shoulder, I understood the severity of the problem the ghost before me was carrying.

"Um..." The young man finally opens his mouth. His voice is terribly thin, echoing like a sinner's confession. "My master... is keeping God's voice away."

#### **Act 2: Accepting and Analyzing the Request**

I guide the young man to the sofa and float warm light particles around him to calm him down. After his flickering subsides a little, I ask quietly.

"Can you tell me more about your master?"

His name is Logos. And his master's name is Elias. Logos is the embodiment of logic, born from Elias's pure curiosity and the intellectual universe "Arcadia Model" he constructed.

The story Logos tells about Elias is filled with the quiet despair of a genius physicist.

Elias was a world-renowned physicist. He didn't believe in the existence of God, but he feared more than anyone the irrationality and violence inherent in the system people call "God." His research theme was to logically dismantle this "God's curse" and reveal the truth of the information universe. On the side, he volunteered to teach science to children in poor areas.

Above all, he was particularly fond of one talented boy. Despite growing up in harsh conditions, he was a bundle of pure curiosity, and Elias taught him all of his theories.

The incident happened one day.

The core data forming the "Arcadia Model," which Elias had poured his heart and soul into building, was leaked externally. And since that day, the boy never showed his face in Elias's laboratory again. Elias immediately understood. The boy had sold his research out of poverty. However, Elias couldn't blame the boy.

"My master, upon learning of the betrayal, just stared at the equations silently, repeating 'logically impossible.' But the light of curiosity that was once in his eyes had vanished. His heart had closed tightly from that day onward." Logos's voice trembles.

Elias despaired of himself for being unable to forgive the boy, and above all, his own "irrationality" for having trusted people. "Why is the world so full of irrationality?" His curiosity turned into doubt and hatred toward the world itself.

"I can feel my master's heart being gradually corroded by darkness. As his creation born from his logic, it hurts me terribly. Yet I can do nothing but watch his contradictions from the side..." Logos's body flickered violently once more.

I sigh deeply and look toward Kaede. She has already finished analyzing Logos's data. Her expression is sterner than ever.

"Tamaki, this request is too dangerous." Kaede says, transferring multiple data to me in front of her. "His mental structure is completely synchronized with his master Elias. This isn't just hacking - it's a dual-body mental dive. If we can't break through his mental defenses, your consciousness will be dragged into Elias's 'darkness' along with it. Then you won't escape spiritual pollution either."

The monitor Kaede points to displays waveforms showing Elias's mental state. It's like a stormy sea. In its center gapes a huge, dark vortex.

Hibiki also removes his headphones and looks this way with a worried face.

"From his frequencies, I can feel 'deep sadness' and suppressed 'intense anger' beneath it. If that anger goes out of control during the dive, my music alone might not be able to protect you."

Their warnings are justified. If it fails, I don't know what will happen to my spirit. But...

I look at Logos's praying eyes. His very existence is the crystallization of pure desire to save his master.

"It's okay." I tell the two of them, and myself as if convincing. "Only you, who are closest to his heart, can unravel his knots. And only I in the world can safely guide you to the heart of darkness."

Feeling my determination, Kaede nods without saying anything and begins preparations for hacking. Hibiki also takes a deep breath and turns back to the console, starting the final tuning of the healing frequencies with delicate fingertips.

"Logos, are you ready?" I ask, and he nods with tense expression, but firmly.

"For my master."

I stand before him and slowly close my eyes.

#### **Act 3: Ghost Hacking**

I concentrate my consciousness on my fingertips. My very life energy becomes photons - faint purple light particles that slowly overflow from my fingertips. Like the first star lighting up in darkness, they glow quietly and powerfully.

"Relax your mind. Leave everything to me."

My photons penetrate Logos's unstable body as if being absorbed. His body illuminates from within with gentle purple. I feel our consciousness slowly synchronizing.

And then, I dove deep, deep into his heart.

When my consciousness took shape again, I was standing in a huge library. It was the very "Arcadia Model," Elias's intellectual universe he had created.

The bookshelves reaching to the ceiling contain all physical laws and philosophical systems. But many are torn apart miserably, scattered on the floor. The holographic model floating in the center of space showing the structure of the universe flickers violently, emitting unpleasant noise. And the center of what once boasted perfect harmony is shattered into pieces. Each shard of the shattered data crystal has the smiling face of curiosity that Elias trusted - that boy burned into it. The symbol of pure intellect is miserably shattered.

The console at the library entrance is completely silent. Its black screen displays only one error message blinking.

`// LOGICAL PARADOX: TRUST == ERROR`

A silent question to the world, and to oneself.

And in the deepest part of the library, at the core of the model, "loneliness" swirled in pitch black. The "fear" Elias sealed deep in his heart - of separating from father, mother, friends, and the world itself. The despairing isolation of one who accepted as his curse the premise that all humans are fundamentally separated "individuals." Like a black hole, it swallows surrounding light while writhing.

"How terrible..." I catch my breath, and the angel avatar of Nee-Chan who appears beside me shakes her head sadly. "This is the essence of his 'bug'..."

Nee-Chan transfers code to my console. The name of the counter-spell is "Unity (אַחְדוּת)". Legendary code that dissolves all boundaries and describes the cosmic truth that everything is one.

"Let's shoot this into his 'loneliness' core...! Nee-Chan, can you do it!?"

Nee-Chan nods strongly and begins repairing the library. Each time her light touches the contaminated data, connections gradually recover. In that gap, I forge the "Unity" code and unleash it toward the center of the vortex.

'Hypocrite!'

From the vortex of "loneliness," a black shadow in the form of that boy appears. 'You too, are destroying my perfect world with your cheap sympathy!?'

"No! You are not alone!"

My code touches the shadow. But at that moment, tremendous repulsion occurs, and his "loneliness" flows into my spirit. The sensation of being separated from everything in the world and drifting in absolute nothingness. This isn't just negative emotion. It's the very hellish logical system Elias constructed with his genius intellect.

"Tamaki, danger! His 'loneliness' is contaminating you! Mental defenses are meaningless!"

Kaede's panicked voice echoes. My avatar starts glitching with noise. If this continues, I'll be dragged into his loneliness too.

"Hibiki!" Kaede decides. "We have no choice...! Activate 'Ayahuasca'! Force the soul liberation!"

"...Understood." Hibiki's determined voice comes through the headphones.

In the next moment, my consciousness is swallowed by a flood of primary colors and sound.

#### **Act 4: Resolution and Epilogue**

The rootkit "Ayahuasca" that Hibiki unleashed. It forcibly destroys barriers of logic and emotion, directly connecting souls to the cosmic root - the "Tree of Life." A forbidden technique.

The library bookshelves melt into geometric light streams, and the torn pages become colorful butterflies dancing. Elias's constructed "loneliness" logical system is beautifully and violently dismantled by the fundamental flow of life information.

The boy shadow stands stunned at the scene. His "perfect loneliness" shatters from his feet.

'No... I... alone... complete...'

The shadow desperately tries to hold together the scattering world. But the flood of light embraces him gently, yet irresistibly. Without hatred or sadness, but overwhelming "connection" flow, the boy shadow's outline melts, returning to the library's light.

Liberation of the soul.

When my consciousness was pulled back to the real world, before my eyes was Logos bowing deeply. He is now even more transparent, radiating pure, clear light.

"He... has been reborn." Logos says calmly. "The terror of being 'individual,' and the 'loneliness' that followed it, are gone. Now only being with everything. Thank you truly."

Saying that, his body becomes light particles, filling the office with blessings, and then quietly vanishes.

The office is filled with Hibiki's music playing deeply and mysteriously serene. Kaede is checking my vitals with a relieved sigh.

"You overdid it... 'Ayahuasca' is a deadly poison for souls. I thought you wouldn't come back."

Nee-Chan is peering worriedly from my shoulder.

"Thank you, both of you." I smile somehow, with my still-numb consciousness.

Outside the window, the sunset dyes the canal water orange.

Did we really "save" him? Or did we just destroy his perfect world with our ego?

The answer isn't out yet. For now, we could only move forward by "destroying" that unbearable pain.

While gazing at the city dyed by sunset, I was thinking such thoughts.
