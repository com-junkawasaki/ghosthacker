The canal is blue. The warmth at my fingertips traces the window and melts into the city through the glass. The scar of the dismantled elevated line has turned green, and a soft wind follows the canal. A lattice of light is woven across the city—there is a quiet pulse.

I settle into feeling good—place a single breath and confirm my footing. I drop anchor into the Tree of Life. Start here.

At my feet, I tap a one-beat kagura. A signal. Nei-chan trims ASC control to match, and the resolution of awareness rises, gently.

Kaede’s voice comes onto comms. Calm as always, but with a trace of tension.

Kaede: “Contamination level is climbing…”

She leaves a small pause.

Kaede: “Tamaki, go carefully. In ER terms, this is ‘watchful caution.’”

Hibiki’s frequency flows into the room—warm, soft overtones.

Hibiki: “Tudo bem, Tamaki. I’ve tuned the prescription of sound.”

His voice carries its usual lightness.

The air loosens. Nei-chan fans a wing of light on my shoulder.

Nei-chan: “Mm. Now you can slip in quietly.”

A translucent call arrives; fine noise scatters into the room. “Triage, ToL,” Nei-chan whispers.

I draw a circle with my fingertip and accept. A young man in a black suit appears—Logos. The brighter the light around his outline, the darker the shadow, like a drawing that can’t decide its lines.

Nei-chan whispers only into my awareness: “Tama, he smells very ‘correct.’ So why does it hurt?”

“He is… Elias.”

The moment a name is granted, the world gains edges. I nod and review the procedure: listen / align / dive. Don’t skip steps. That’s the shortest path to fewer accidents.

Nei-chan adds: “His ghost is terrified of bodily death and the severance of gene—terrified that ‘you’ as a subject will vanish. That seems to be the core of his bug.”

“Death isn’t an end. It’s just change,” I exhale softly. “We carry our memes forward through children, through culture, through society. That is how we are released from loneliness. His pain is that he can no longer perceive those connections.”

The Ghost Hacker’s work is to free such hardened memes and return them to flow.

I tap kagura once more. Open the throttle a touch. Nei-chan: “Received.”

The room grows quiet.

Hibiki’s low band glides along the floor—gentle, reassuring.

Kaede lays down security rails. ER quick, ER sure.

I send thanks with a glance.

Feeling good is mostly teamwork. Not forcing it alone is, sometimes, the bravest thing.

“Let’s go, Logos.”

“Tamaki, I… want to save my lord.”

Desire is an arrow. When arrows align, dives are stable. I close my eyes and gather photons into my fingertips.

Behind me, there is a quiet bug—loneliness justified by reason. I do not forget it.
