# EP1-2 — The Non-Separation Code and the Library (Dive)
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Dive / Confrontation / Trigger

When my awareness regains shape, I’m on the landing of a library. Stacks that reach the sky. Shattered data crystals. In the center, a single line of error:

// LOGICAL PARADOX: TRUST == ERROR

Elias’s loneliness is made of logic. The fear of being unable to define the end of the self hardens into correctness, becomes a wall, and seals the entrance to breath. I deploy the non-separation code I received from Nei-chan—vocabularies that dissolve the boundary called “individual” and reconnect to the flow of memes—and cast it into the whirlpool’s core. Language that loosens boundaries usually sounds gentle.

The pushback is instant. A shadow appears and presses vacuum into my chest. Static snow scours my vision; my knees waver.

Far off, Kaede’s voice—tense but composed.

Kaede: “Tamaki, come back!”

Quick, like an ER doctor.

Kaede: “I’ll break that logic-camouflage from outside. Hibiki, sending coordinates—counter-frequency!”

Nei-chan trembles: “Tama… is he right that trust is an error? Logically…?”

Not ‘don’t go back’—go back while advancing. I re-sync my breathing to feeling good and say it to Nei-chan and to myself. Don’t choose fear. Fear narrows the field and steals care.

I tap a small kagura at my feet. Signal. The throttle comes up half a step.

“Hibiki, ready.”

Hibiki answers, more serious than usual: “OK. Give me the cue, Tamaki.”

I lay my palm on the core of the vortex and quietly rename it. Not “loneliness,” just pain. Change the name and the hardness changes a bit.

“Now.”

Primary colors surge. Hibiki’s ayahuasca undoes the knot of space.

Pages become butterflies and dance; the shadow loses its outline and returns to light.

I rise, dwelling in the quiet.

Feeling good returns.

Nei-chan: “Hehe, we came back.”

Elias places a hand on his brow and practices a slow smile. Logos’s wings tremble. I place a “non-separation pact” between them and hand over homework for the next week. Far off, the reason that justifies loneliness creaks.

I rest my fingers on the landing’s rail. Warmth returns.

Nei-chan: “ASC, hold. Tama, next?”

“Next. Half a step.”

Page-shadow rustles. A small inversion.

Kaede returns to comms, worry in her tone.

Kaede: “Tamaki, come back.”

“I am. Coming back while going on.”

I tap a one-beat kagura. The noise in my vision drops a level.

Elias whispers: “I’ve been protecting myself with ‘correctness.’”

“Yeah. Correctness matters. But breath comes first.”

Hibiki’s low tones flow in—warm, kind.

The eddies loosen.

The shadow darkens for a beat—resistance—then thins. Small wins and losses alternate.

I laugh, short: “Good. That’s change.”

Nei-chan lowers a light: “Tama, another half-step?”

“Half-step. Up to here.”

Logos places a hand to his chest: “My lord is still far.”

“Not far. The path is already here. Match stride.”

I add a line to my notebook: Repeat ‘half-step forward’ three times.

Kaede returns, voice back to calm: “Shall we… cut it here for today?”

“Yeah. Cut. Return. We’ll dive next time.”

The wind on the landing shifts. The canal’s presence hums faintly in the stacks.

I meet Elias’s gaze and nod. “Let’s continue. Slowly is fine.”
