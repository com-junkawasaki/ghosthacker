Lower level of the Shibuya canal, Schwa’s operating room. Black walls, low luminance. Hibiki’s frequency flows faintly through the space.

Today is Millia’s surgery to remove the ‘adhesion of soul (Complex).’

Kaede: “Pressure is… stable this morning.”

Calm voice, laced with tension.

Kaede: “But we’re diving deep. I’ve been present at many spiritual surgeries… it’s nerve-wracking every time.”

Hibiki: “Tudo bem, Kaede. I’ve fully isolated the room’s band. Zero external noise. Sound, light—everything tuned for Millia’s healing.”

He’s more serious than usual, still warm.

Nei-chan: “Tama, they say it hurts. Are you okay?”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “I’m okay. We’re ready.”

Kaede: “...Pain will come. We are cutting soul.” She lowers her gaze.

Kaede: “But the procedure is exact. We all protect Millia. Tamaki, you guide. Hibiki, as anesthesiologist, carries ‘anesthesia of awareness.’ Dr. Itako operates.”

ER-calm, with deep compassion.

Tamaki: “Understood.”

I stand at the center. This is an OR for spirit, not flesh. The pain is real.

The door opens.

Millia and Leo enter.

Millia’s eyes hold resolve and fear; her fingertips tremble.

Leo cups her hand; worry on his face.

Nei-chan: “Tama, both are tense. But they’re ready.”

Hibiki steps forward. The badge of anesthesia glints on his coat.

He meets Millia’s eyes with a gentle smile.

Hibiki: “Millia, first tell me your intention. Why do you dive today?”

Soft voice with professional gravity.

Hibiki: “Ayahuasca is not just anesthesia. Your intention sets the vector of healing.”

Millia breathes in.

Millia: “...To cut the ‘chain of house’ I inherited from my mother.”

Trembling voice, with resolve.

Millia: “To live my freedom.”

Hibiki nods deeply, eyes warm with respect.

Hibiki: “Muito bem. Don’t forget that intention. I’ll protect your awareness and lead you gently into depth.”

He signals to Kaede.

Consent appears in hologram, blue-white light filling the room.

Kaede: “Final consent before operation.”

ER-calm, warm.

Kaede: “Anesthesia (Ayahuasca protocol), installation of informational field, intraoperative guidance, handling of records. I’ll read each, slowly. Millia, stop anytime to ask.”

She reads, carefully.

Millia listens intently. Leo nods by her side.

Reading complete, Millia overlaps hands to sign—trembling, without hesitation.

Leo signs the support consent. Resolve and worry mix on his face.

Kaede: “...Thank you. We will protect you with everything.”

Hibiki: “Then, I’ll begin anesthesia.”

He meets her gaze.

Hibiki: “I’ll install Ayahuasca code into the information field. You will ingest. But this is not coercion. You choose.”

Millia nods.

He pauses, smiling gently.

Hibiki: “—Before that, three breaths. With me. Matching breath calms the heart.”

He inhales, slow and warm.

Millia follows.

One breath—

Slow, deep.

Two breaths—

Deeper.

Three breaths—

The air in the room shifts.

A small device glows in Hibiki’s hand.

Blue-white light fills the room.

A vine-like UI unfurls in the information space.

Code is woven, quietly.

Beautiful, and a little uncanny.

Nei-chan: “Tama, this… is very complex.”

Hibiki: “I’ll be DJ through the operation—continuously generating the field.”

Quiet, firm; eyes in deep focus.

Hibiki: “Sound, light, scent—everything will support your healing. I’ll protect your awareness as anesthesiologist, while I weave the field with music.”

He smiles a little.

Hibiki: “Do not fear. I’ll be listening to your breath the whole time.”

Millia nods—resolve, and a trace of relief.

She takes the cup in both hands. Warm.

Hibiki: “Millia… do you choose to drink now?”

Gentle.

She thinks a little, then says with resolve:

Millia: “...Yes. I choose it.”

She drinks.

Her throat bobs.

Bitterness spreads across her tongue.

Leo supports her shoulder.

Leo: “It’s okay. I’m here.”

Nei-chan squeezes my hand.

Nei-chan: “Tama, it begins.”

I tap a small kagura, raise ASC.

The air changes.

Dr. Itako emerges from the back.

Young, speaking old; a glamorous elder.

“Patient, good stability. Hibiki, phase of the field?”

Hibiki: “Stable. Gentle descent to alpha. Gamma suppressed.”

The room pulses low.

Millia’s outline trembles.

A black ‘chain’ appears behind her.

Nei-chan: “Tama, that’s…”

Dr. Itako raises a scalpel of light and smiles slightly.

“I usually do sutures, but fine, I’ll cut this time.”

Confidence, easy.

Dr. Itako: “Tamaki, hold the position of awareness.”

I nod.

“Millia, I’m here. Do not fear. We go together.”

She meets my eyes—trust.

—The operation begins.
