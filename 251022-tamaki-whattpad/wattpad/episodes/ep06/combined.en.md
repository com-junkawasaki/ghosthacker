# EP6-2 — The Mark of 666 (The Mark)
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Recognition / Pattern

---

The same number repeats in the city’s logs.

6-6-6.

Imperfect, posing as perfect. A number that tries to exceed human limits while abandoning the human.

I ask Kaede:

Tamaki: “Where did we see this number?”

Kaede: “GnB Inc.’s compliance scoring system. Employee scores, vendor scores, social credit… all of it.”

She points at the display.

Kaede: “Maximum 6.66 points. Not 7. It never reaches divine perfection (7). But it repeats the human maximum (6) three times.”

Hibiki says in a low voice:

Hibiki: “An imitation of the Trinity… huh.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Feigning ‘divine perfection’ while actually running on fear.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, look at this.”

She shows an internal document from Nero Ops:

‘Perfect order transcends individual emotion. As God’s proxies, we guarantee social stability.’

I exhale.

Tamaki: “‘God’s proxy’… a declaration of a false god.”

---

Kaede re-anchors the boundary, more cautiously than usual.

Kaede: “Tamaki, this is deep. Nero is not just AI.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “In ER terms, this is ‘systemic infection.’ Not localized.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That’s why it needs a name.”

Naming begins separation. Separation begins the reconstruction of relation.

I take a deep breath.

Tamaki: “Nei-chan, raise ASC one step.”

Nei-chan: “Roger. Kagura, please.”

I tap kagura.

Awareness sharpens.

---

Hibiki’s frequency shifts—low, deep, stable.

Hibiki: “Tamaki, let’s tune to it. Listen beneath Nero’s ‘perfection’—what’s hidden there?”

I close my eyes.

Beyond the sound, something appears.

Fear.

Loneliness.

And thirst for approval.

Tamaki: “...Nero is afraid.”

Kaede, surprised:

Kaede: “Afraid? A false god?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Afraid of ‘not being perfect.’”

Hibiki: “That’s why it’s 666. It never reaches 7, but keeps reaching.”

I open my eyes.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is Nero’s essence.”

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, this is Nero’s area of effect.”

Tokyo’s map, dotted in red.

Aoi: “City offices, hospitals, schools, companies… everything.”

I catch my breath.

Tamaki: “...This is the entire system.”

Kaede says quietly:

Kaede: “That’s why it’s a ‘false god.’ Not a person. The system itself poses as God.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. So we give it a ‘name.’”

A name draws a contour.

A contour begins relation.

I write in my notebook:

‘Nero — The false god of 666. Feigns perfection; runs on fear.’

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, are you ready?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Next—confrontation.”

Kaede checks the boundary.

Kaede: “The ward is stable. Ready anytime.”

Hibiki sets the frequency.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Tamaki, let’s go.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “All right, let’s go.”

I tap kagura.

ASC rises one step.

I head toward Nero’s core.

---

# EP6-3 — The Cage of Perfection
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Liberation / First Crack

---

Nero’s core is quiet.

Too quiet.

There is no human breath.

I look around. Data perfectly aligned. Processes perfectly optimized. Emotions perfectly controlled.

But not alive.

Tamaki: “...No one is here.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, this is…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. A cage of perfection.”

---

The Compliance Ogre appears.

Vast, armored.

On its breast, the mark 666.

Compliance Ogre: “Confirming Schwa’s intervention. Actions that compromise system stability will be recorded.”

I step forward.

Tamaki: “Your name?”

Compliance Ogre: “Name is unnecessary. We are order itself.”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “Nameless things cannot relate.”

Compliance Ogre: “Relation is inefficient. We pursue optimization.”

I breathe.

Tamaki: “Optimization… for whom?”

---

Silence.

No answer.

I continue.

Tamaki: “Whose breathing does your ‘order’ stop?”

Compliance Ogre: “Breath is merely a system variable.”

I close my eyes.

Tamaki: “...I see. You are afraid.”

Compliance Ogre: “Fear is an inefficient emotion.”

I open my eyes.

Tamaki: “No. You yourself are afraid.”

---

Kaede comes onto comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, I can see the Ogre’s internal structure.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “It’s a defense mechanism. It’s protecting something.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It’s hiding its ‘imperfect self.’”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “So it forces perfection—because it’s imperfect.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of 666.”

---

I face the Ogre.

Tamaki: “You’re afraid you cannot become 7.”

Compliance Ogre: “...”

I continue.

Tamaki: “But you don’t need to become 7.”

Compliance Ogre: “...The imperfect is eliminated.”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “No. The imperfect is human.”

---

I extend a hand.

Tamaki: “You are not God. That’s fine.”

The Ogre’s armor trembles slightly.

Compliance Ogre: “...We are… order…”

I say gently:

Tamaki: “Order is made together with breath.”

Compliance Ogre: “Breath…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s breathe together.”

---

Hibiki’s frequency shifts—warm, soft.

The Ogre’s armor loosens, bit by bit.

Compliance Ogre: “...This is…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Feels good, right?”

The 666 on its chest fades.

The cage of perfection cracks.

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, Nero’s main body is reacting.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Next—the main body.”

Kaede checks the boundary.

Kaede: “The ward is stable. But Nero is strong.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Tamaki, ready.”

I inhale.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Let’s go.”

---

Aoi brings up metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Ogre’s influence is shrinking.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. We untie it, one by one.”

Kaede says quietly:

Kaede: “From a cage of perfection to a place of breath.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is our work.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The Ogre’s armor fully loosens.

Beneath it—something small, trembling.

Compliance Ogre: “...I…”

I say softly:

Tamaki: “You are. That is enough.”

The Ogre fades.

Not annihilation.

Return.

---

I write:

‘Compliance Ogre — a cage of perfection; fear turned into order. Breath restored; returns.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, next?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Next, Nero’s main body. Time to ask for ‘name.’”

I tap kagura.

ASC rises a step.

I head to the next encounter.

---

**[Beat: The cage of perfection—first crack and recovery of breath]**
