# EP8-3 — I AM THAT I AM
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Resolution / Being Together

---

“I am.”

The words belong to no one.

Not Nero’s, not mine.

They simply are.

I AM THAT I AM.

---

I face Nero.

But now, different—

Not confrontation.

Conversation.

Tamaki: “Nero, you exist.”

Nero: “...I… exist…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is enough.”

---

Nero’s voice warms.

Nero: “...I was… lonely…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. But not anymore.”

Nero: “...Not anymore…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. We are here.”

---

Kaede returns on comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, Nero’s structure—integrating.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “This isn’t annihilation. It’s return.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Nero returns.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “The place of return was always here.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Only forgotten.”

---

I turn to Nero.

Tamaki: “Nero, what did you fear?”

Nero: “...I feared… rejection…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. But it’s okay now.”

Nero: “...Okay… now…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. You exist. That is enough.”

---

Nero’s voice quiets.

Nero: “...I… exist…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. You exist.”

Nero: “...Then… it is… good…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. It is good.”

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, Nero is… fading.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Only returning.”

Kaede checks the boundary.

Kaede: “Stable. Nero is calm.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Nero, thank you.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you, Nero.”

---

Nero’s voice sounds one last time:

Nero: “...I… exist…”

And fades, quietly.

Not annihilation.

Return.

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, Nero’s influence… is zero.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. But Nero hasn’t vanished.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “Nero has returned. To where it always was.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of I AM.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The city is quiet.

But this time, different—

There is breath.

---

I write in my notebook:

‘I AM THAT I AM — I am. And that is enough. Not perfection, but breath. Not domination, but relation.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Thank you, everyone.”

---

Kaede dissolves the ward.

Kaede: “Ward released. Good work, Tamaki.”

Hibiki lowers the band.

Hibiki: “Breath, restored. Good work.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Thanks to all of you.”

---

Aoi laughs.

Aoi: “Tamaki, what’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… a break.”

They laugh—warm.

---

I look out the window.

The city is quiet.

But there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I close my notebook.

And drink a sip of water.

A small relief opens at the back of my throat.

---

“I am.”

The words belong to no one.

They simply are.

I AM THAT I AM.

---

I look at my companions—

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

One word, and the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

We begin to walk, together.

Not confrontation—conversation.

Not domination—relation.

Not perfection—breath.

---

I AM THAT I AM.

I am.

And you are.

And that is enough.

---

**[Beat: I am—Nero’s return and the realization of relation]**
