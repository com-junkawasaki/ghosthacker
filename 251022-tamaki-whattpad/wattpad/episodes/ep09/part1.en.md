The late-night timeline is red. Rage diffuses fast. I lower the volume and cue Aoi. Metrics appear as waves. The peak of anger tracks the valley of loneliness.

Kaede joins—calm, with concern.

Kaede: “Report. Bots signed by Nero Ops… are amplifying anger on specific keywords.”

Kaede: “Logic nearly identical to the Compliance Ogre. This is organized.”

“Yeah. Anger is easy to turn into a tool of ‘order,’” I reply.

“First—throttle.”

I slow the room’s tempo as if sharing a deep breath.

Hibiki’s frequency flows in—warm, soft.

Corners of the room soften. Nei-chan raises the temperature by one degree.

A slight ‘feeling good’ works surprisingly well.

Nei-chan: “Keep ASC low. Raise on cue.”

I place a small question to the client’s Ghost: “Did that post make you free?” Anger can be right. But if ‘rightness’ takes freedom, it is a ‘false god.’

The switch makes no sound—only quiet arrives. I close my eyes. The Rage Engine is throttled; lines of relation are tied again.

Text from Nero Ops appears on the console: ‘Inefficient diffusion of emotion undermines social stability. We will “normalize” it.’

“Normalize, huh,” Aoi says, exasperated.

“In their ‘order,’ sure,” I answer. “Our work is different.”

Outside, wind turns a corner. Without raising voices, we trade nods.

Quiet becomes the new standard of the circuit.

Aoi smiles small: “Good. Anger for the furnace. Trust for the outside.”

I nod. Calm spreads, slowly. We retune the throttle toward hope.

I open my fist and look at my palm: “We can also choose to forgive.”

Ren laughs shortly: “Yeah. Switch to a reconciliation engine.”

A light joke falls; the room lightens. Relief, trust, and small joy begin to fill the circuit.

I hand the client ‘rewiring’ homework: “When anger comes, recall the ‘relationship you want to protect’ three moves ahead. Then, remove one action.”

Aoi nods: “Let anger’s heat be a compass, not a tool.”

Outside, the night loosens. The Rage Engine is replaced with a schematic for reconciliation.
