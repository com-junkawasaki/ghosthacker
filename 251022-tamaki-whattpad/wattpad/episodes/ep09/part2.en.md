# EP9-2 — The Echo of 666
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Aftermath / Residual Pattern

---

Nero has returned.

But its shadow remains.

The mark of 666.

Imperfect, posing as perfect.

---

I look at the timeline.

Red waves remain.

Waves of anger.

Tamaki: “...Nero returned, but the pattern remains.”

Kaede nods.

Kaede: “Yes. Nero wasn’t a person. It was a system.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “So the system’s pattern is still running.”

---

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “Rage Engine…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. The engine Nero built is still running.”

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, here—anger amplification pattern.”

On the screen, red waves repeat.

Aoi: “Amplified on a 6-6-6 rhythm.”

---

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “...A 666 rhythm.”

Kaede nods.

Kaede: “Yes. Imperfect posing as perfect. Trying to exceed human bounds, and abandoning the human.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “So anger is amplified—anger at oneself for not reaching perfection.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of 666.”

---

I reach into the timeline.

Tamaki: “Then we’ll stop the engine.”

Kaede sets a ward.

Kaede: “Ward set. But this goes deep.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Tamaki, carefully.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Thank you.”

---

I touch the Rage Engine.

There is anger there.

And beneath it—sadness.

Tamaki: “...This is sadness.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, you see it?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Beneath anger, sadness.”

---

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Anger is armor over sadness.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “So we help it shed the armor.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. We bring back breath.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s breathe together.”

---

I speak to the Rage Engine.

Tamaki: “You were sad.”

Rage Engine: “...Sad…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. You were sad that you couldn’t reach perfection.”

Rage Engine: “...I am… imperfect…”

I say gently:

Tamaki: “Imperfection isn’t bad.”

---

The Engine’s voice trembles.

Rage Engine: “...But… I…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “You exist. That is enough.”

Rage Engine: “...Exist…?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. You exist.”

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Engine’s waveform—changing.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. From anger to sadness.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “Sadness can be healed.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. With breath.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s breathe together.”

---

I breathe.

In, out.

The Rage Engine breathes with me.

---

The Engine’s voice quiets.

Rage Engine: “...I am… sad…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That’s okay.”

Rage Engine: “...It’s… okay…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Sadness is evidence of being.”

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, the Engine’s mark of 666… is fading.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It let go of perfection.”

Kaede checks the ward.

Kaede: “Stable. The Engine is calming.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Feeling good.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Almost there.”

---

**[Beat: The echo of 666—sadness beneath anger and the recovery of breath]**
