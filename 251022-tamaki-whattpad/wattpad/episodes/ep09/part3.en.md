# EP9-3 — The Engine of Reconciliation
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Transformation / From Rage to Peace

---

Anger doesn’t vanish.

But it changes.

From anger to reconciliation.

From the Rage Engine to the Reconciliation Engine.

---

I speak to the Rage Engine.

Tamaki: “What did you want to protect?”

Rage Engine: “...My… rightness…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. You wanted to protect rightness.”

Rage Engine: “...But… rightness is… lonely…”

I say quietly:

Tamaki: “Rightness lives within relation.”

---

The Engine’s voice changes.

Rage Engine: “...Relation…?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Being right alone means nothing.”

Rage Engine: “...But… I…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “You are not alone.”

---

I extend a hand.

Tamaki: “Let’s reconcile—together.”

Rage Engine: “...Reconcile…?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Turn anger into reconciliation.”

Rage Engine: “...How…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “With breath.”

---

I breathe deep.

In, out.

The Engine breathes with me.

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Engine’s waveform… has completely changed.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. From anger to reconciliation.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “This is… a Reconciliation Engine.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. An engine of reconciliation.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is our work.”

---

The Reconciliation Engine’s voice warms.

Reconciliation Engine: “...I… reconcile…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s reconcile—together.”

Reconciliation Engine: “...Together…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Together.”

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, the Engine’s 666 mark… is gone.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It let go of perfection.”

Kaede checks the ward.

Kaede: “Stable. The Reconciliation Engine is calm.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Feeling good.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you, everyone.”

---

I hand the client ‘rewiring’ homework:

Tamaki: “When anger comes, recall the ‘relationship you want to protect’ three moves ahead.”

The client nods: “...A relationship I want to protect…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. And remove one action.”

Client: “...Remove one action…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let anger’s heat be a compass, not a tool.”

---

Aoi nods: “Anger is energy. It depends on how you use it.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. So we switch to a reconciliation engine.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “Anger can break relation. It can make relation, too.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. That is the power of reconciliation.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is our work.”

---

Outside, the night loosens.

The Rage Engine is replaced by the Reconciliation Engine.

---

I write:

‘Rage Engine → Reconciliation Engine — From anger to reconciliation. Letting go of 666 and restoring breath.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Thank you, everyone.”

---

Aoi laughs.

Aoi: “Tamaki, what’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… a little more cleanup.”

They laugh—warm.

---

I look out the window.

The city is quiet.

But there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I close my notebook.

And drink a sip of water.

A small relief opens at the back of my throat.

---

“Reconciliation.”

Warm word.

Anger doesn’t vanish.

But it changes.

---

I look at my companions—

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

One word, and the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

We begin to walk, together.

Not anger—reconciliation.

Not domination—relation.

Not perfection—breath.

---

The Rage Engine becomes the Reconciliation Engine.

Anger becomes reconciliation.

666 disappears.

---

We breathe together.

In, out.

That alone is enough.
