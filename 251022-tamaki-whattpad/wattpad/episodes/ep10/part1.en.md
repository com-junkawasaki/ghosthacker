A storm of notifications. Not someone’s will—someone’s ‘cleanup’ stuck in an infinite loop.

I start a program in Schwa’s console: Zombie Sweeper.

Kaede sets a ward—her usual quick precision.

Aoi expands the visualization.

Kaede joins, calm with a touch of irony:

Kaede: “GnB Inc. has released a PR statement.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “‘Unnecessary information loops damage social soundness; we clean them as part of routine hygiene management.’”

Me: “‘Cleaning,’ huh. Unnecessary to whom, I wonder.”

The key is not ‘discarding.’ While piping unwanted loops to /dev/null, we protect the relational lines that should remain. I touch the Tree of Life and execute each step.

The enemy is a ‘zombie loop’—a norm of exclusion belonging to no one. I unhook them one by one.

Aoi: “ASC?”

Me: “Keep it low. Raise on cue.”

Aoi: “Do we keep this tag?”

Me: “Yes. It will be a marker for someone to find their way back.”

Cleaning means leaving a path home. Morning announcements play; the city’s noise drops half a step.

On console, an official notice from GnB Inc. PR: ‘We acknowledge your intervention. Any interference with our social hygiene program will be recorded.’

Aoi shrugs: “There’s that ‘record’ again.”

Me: “Mm. Our record is here.” I point to my chest.

“Welcome back.”

Morning comes; the city is a little quiet. Quiet is not boredom. It’s like breath. I rest a hand on my chest and prepare for the next request.

Before I open the door, I take a sip of water. A small ease opens at the back of my throat.
