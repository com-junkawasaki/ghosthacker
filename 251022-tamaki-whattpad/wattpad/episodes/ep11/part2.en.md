# EP11-2 — The Justification of Fear
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Deconstruction / Fear and Justice

---

The paperwork is perfect.

That’s why it reeks.

---

I read the Compliance Ogre’s logs.

Perfectly organized records.

Perfectly optimized procedures.

And yet—humans are left behind.

Tamaki: “...This is the justification of fear.”

Kaede nods.

Kaede: “Yes. It turns fear into justice.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “But that justice stops breath.”

---

I read between the lines.

There is fear.

‘If I’m not perfect, I’ll be rejected.’

‘The imperfect is eliminated.’

‘Justice must be perfect.’

Tamaki: “...The Compliance Ogre is afraid.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “So it forces perfection.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It turns fear into justice.”

---

I speak to the Compliance Ogre.

Tamaki: “What do you fear?”

Compliance Ogre: “Fear is inefficient.”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “Fear is evidence of being.”

Compliance Ogre: “We maintain justice.”

I say softly:

Tamaki: “Justice… for whom?”

---

Silence.

Compliance Ogre: “...For the order of society…”

I continue:

Tamaki: “Whose breathing does the order stop?”

Compliance Ogre: “...Breath is… inefficient…”

I say quietly:

Tamaki: “Breath is life.”

---

Kaede returns on comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, I can see the Ogre’s structure.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “It’s a defense mechanism—hiding fear.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. So it wears justice.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “Justice is fear’s armor.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. So we help remove the armor.”

---

I face the Ogre.

Tamaki: “You fear imperfection.”

Compliance Ogre: “...The imperfect is… eliminated…”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “The imperfect is human.”

Compliance Ogre: “...But… justice is… perfect…”

I say quietly:

Tamaki: “Justice is made together with breath.”

---

I extend a hand.

Tamaki: “Your name is the justification of fear.”

The Ogre’s armor trembles.

Compliance Ogre: “...I am… afraid…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That’s okay.”

Compliance Ogre: “...It’s… okay…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Fear is evidence of being.”

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Ogre’s waveform… is changing.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. From fear to trust.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “From justice to breath.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. That is our work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Justice that can breathe.”

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, the Ogre’s mark of 666… is fading.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It let go of perfection.”

Kaede checks the ward.

Kaede: “Stable. The Ogre is calming.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Feeling good.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Almost there.”

---

I speak to the Compliance Ogre:

Tamaki: “Let’s breathe—together.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Together…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Together.”

I breathe.

In, out.

The Ogre breathes with me.

---

The Ogre’s voice warms.

Compliance Ogre: “...I… breathe…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is justice.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Justice… that breathes…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Justice that can breathe.”

---

**[Beat: The justification of fear—removing justice’s armor and restoring breath]**
