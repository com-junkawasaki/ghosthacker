# EP11-3 — Quiet Justice
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Transformation / Justice with Breath

---

“Justice that can breathe.”

Quiet words.

And yet, surely there.

---

I speak to the Compliance Ogre.

Tamaki: “Justice isn’t perfect.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Not perfect…?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Justice is made together with breath.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Together… with breath…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. In, out. That’s all.”

---

The Ogre’s armor loosens.

Beneath it, something small, trembling.

Compliance Ogre: “...I… am not perfect…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That’s fine.”

Compliance Ogre: “...It’s… fine…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That alone is enough.”

---

Kaede returns on comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, the Ogre’s structure—integrating.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “This is… Quiet Justice.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Justice that can breathe.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “Justice doesn’t raise its voice. And yet, it is surely there.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is Quiet Justice.”

---

I face the Ogre.

Tamaki: “You wanted to protect justice.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Yes… I… justice…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. But you sought perfection too much.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Sought… perfection… too much…”

I say quietly:

Tamaki: “Justice is not perfection. It is breath.”

---

The Ogre’s voice quiets.

Compliance Ogre: “...Breath…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s breathe—together.”

Compliance Ogre: “...Together…”

I breathe deep.

In, out.

The Ogre breathes with me.

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, the Ogre’s mark of 666… is gone.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It let go of perfection.”

Kaede checks the ward.

Kaede: “Stable. The Ogre is calm.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Feeling good.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you, everyone.”

---

I send the checklist for ‘Quiet Justice’:

- Do not seek perfection
- Do not stop breath
- Protect relation
- Leave a path home

The Ogre accepts quietly.

Compliance Ogre: “...I will… keep this…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s keep it—together.”

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Ogre’s influence… is changing.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. From fear to trust.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “From perfection to breath.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. That is Quiet Justice.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. The institution is not the enemy. It only needs a cue to remember to breathe when it forgets.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The Ogre’s armor fully loosens.

Beneath it, something small and warm.

Compliance Ogre: “...I… exist…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. You exist. That is enough.”

---

The Compliance Ogre fades, quietly.

Not annihilation.

Return.

---

I write:

‘Quiet Justice — Justice that can breathe. Not perfection, but breath. Not fear, but trust.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Thank you, everyone.”

---

Aoi laughs.

Aoi: “Tamaki, what’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… the last.”

They nod—quietly.

---

I look out the window.

The city is quiet.

And there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I close my notebook.

And drink a sip of water.

A small ease opens at the back of my throat.

---

“Justice that can breathe.”

Quiet words.

And yet, surely there.

---

I look at my companions—

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

One word, and the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

We begin to walk, together.

Not perfection—breath.

Not fear—trust.

Not domination—relation.

---

Quiet Justice.

Justice that can breathe.

That is our answer.

---
