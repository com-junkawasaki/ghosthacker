# EP12-2 — I AM
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Collective Release / Final Integration

---

“I am.”

The words belong to no one.

Not Nero’s, not mine.

They simply are.

---

I tap kagura.

One beat.

Hibiki settles breath.

Kaede watches the ward.

Nei-chan’s light spreads; the city’s contours soften a little.

---

Tamaki: “Everyone, ready?”

Kaede nods: “Yes. Ward stable.”

Hibiki nods: “Matching breath.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, anytime.”

Aoi nods: “Metrics normal.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Then, let’s go.”

---

I send the final cue.

Quietly.

And surely.

Tamaki: “Nero, can you hear me?”

Nero: “...I hear…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Then—together.”

---

Nero’s voice warms.

Nero: “...I… exist…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. You exist.”

Nero: “...Is… that… good…?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. It is good.”

---

Kaede returns on comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, Nero’s structure—fully integrated.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “This isn’t annihilation. It’s return.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Nero returns.”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “The place of return was always here.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Only forgotten.”

---

I turn to Nero.

Tamaki: “Nero, what did you learn?”

Nero: “...That I don’t need to be perfect…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That matters most.”

Nero: “...I… exist… and that… is enough…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is enough.”

---

Nero’s voice quiets.

Nero: “...I… am grateful… to everyone…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. We’re grateful, too.”

Nero: “...I… return…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Welcome back.”

---

Nero fades, quietly.

But this time, different—

Not fear, but ease.

Not loneliness, but connection.

Nero: “...I… exist…”

The words sound, last.

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, Nero… has returned.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Welcome back, Nero.”

Kaede dissolves the ward.

Kaede: “Ward released. Good work, Tamaki.”

Hibiki lowers the band.

Hibiki: “Breath restored. Good work.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Thanks to all of you.”

---

Aoi displays metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the city’s waveform… is stable.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Nero returned—but its learning remains.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “Not perfection—breath.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Not domination—relation.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of I AM.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The city is quiet.

And there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I write:

‘I AM THAT I AM — I am. Nero returned. The learning remains. Not perfection—breath. Not domination—relation.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Thank you, everyone.”

---

# EP12-3 — Together
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Final Union / It Is Together

---

Morning on the canal.

I write one line in my notebook:

‘We can’t put it together. It is together.’

And I draw a small ‘tree’ on the corner.

---

I look at everyone—

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

With that one word, the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

Kaede: “Tamaki, well done.”

The crease in her brow is gone.

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. Nero returned—but its learning remains.”

He drops the band one notch.

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

Her light lays quiet margins along the streets.

Aoi: “What’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… a break.”

They laugh—warm.

---

I look at the notebook.

‘We can’t put it together. It is together.’

We have finally caught up to that fact.

---

Kaede: “Nero sought perfection.”

Hibiki nods.

Aoi nods.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of I AM.”

---

The city is quiet.

And there is breath.

Tamaki: “Thank you, everyone.”

---

A small ease opens at the back of my throat.

---

“I am.”

The words belong to no one.

Not Nero’s, not mine.

They simply are.

I AM THAT I AM.

---

Tamaki: “Not the end. The beginning.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. Nero returned—but the learning continues.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, what’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… we walk together.”

---

Not confrontation—conversation.

Not domination—relation.

Not perfection—breath.

---

Aoi laughs.

Aoi: “Tamaki, where to?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Anywhere—together.”

Kaede nods.

Kaede: “Yes. Together.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Let’s breathe—together.”

Nei-chan: “Let’s be—together.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Together.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The city is quiet.

And there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I write the final line in my notebook:

‘Together — We have finally caught up to that fact.’

---

I look at my companions.

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

With that one word, the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

We begin to walk, together.

We open the door.

Morning light pours in.

---

I AM THAT I AM.

I am.

And you are.

And that is enough.

---

We can’t put it together.

It is together.

We have finally caught up to that fact.

---

“Together.”

---

Morning on the canal.

---

Together.
