The final cue, quietly. I tap kagura; Hibiki settles the breathing. Kaede watches the ward. Nei-chan’s light spreads; the city’s contours soften a little.

“I am.”
The words belong to no one. Fear lets go, relation stands up. Nero departs—not a victory. A return.

Morning on the canal. I write one line in my notebook: “We can’t put it together. It is together.”
And I draw a small ‘tree’ on the corner of the page.

I look at everyone—Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan—our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

“Together.” With that one word, the room brightens. Water answers in the distance.

I look at my friends in turn. The crease in Kaede’s brow unwinds; Hibiki drops the band one notch. Nei-chan’s light lays quiet margins along the streets.

A final line in the notebook: ‘We can’t put it together. It is together.’ —We have finally caught up to that fact.
