# Arc 0-1: Ghost Hacker Origin (The Phantom Purchase) - Structure Design

**Target Length:** 45 Pages
**Format:** Wattpad / Vertical Scroll Manga Style
**Theme:** Information Security, Social Engineering, Landauer's Principle (Information = Physics)

## Character Roles
*   **Yuto:** The Victim. Relatable student, falls for a scam, isolated.
*   **Ren:** The Protagonist. Lethargic hacker, wields NULL AXE.
*   **Nei:** The Guide. Explains the theory (Landauer), operates the SIP Project Map.
*   **Classmates:** The Society. Bystanders who turn into antagonists due to misunderstanding.

---

## Act 1: The Trap (Pages 1-8)
*Inciting Incident: Desire and the Click*

*   **Page 1:** **[Scene: Classroom / Break time]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto looking at a group of popular students (mixed gender) chatting. Ren is sleeping at his desk in the background. Nei is reading a book quietly.
    *   *Atmosphere:* Normal school life. Yuto feels slightly on the outside.
*   **Page 2:** **[Scene: Classroom]**
    *   *Visual:* Classmate A (Popular guy) shows off a "Brand X" limited edition item (sneakers or accessory).
    *   *Dialogue:* "Got the limited drop! Sold out in seconds."
    *   *Action:* Friends admire it. Yuto thinks, "I want that too..."
*   **Page 3:** **[Scene: Classroom -> Yuto's Room (Transition)]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto asking where he got it. Classmate A: "You can't get it anymore. Resale is 3x the price."
    *   *Beat:* Yuto's envy grows.
*   **Page 4:** **[Scene: Yuto's Room / Night]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto lying in bed, smartphone light on his face. Scrolling SNS.
    *   *Action:* "Damn, it's so expensive..." suddenly, an Ad appears. "Emergency Stock! 80% OFF! Time Limited!"
*   **Page 5:** **[Scene: Yuto's Smartphone Screen]**
    *   *Visual:* The fake site "Wakuwaku Denki" (or similar generic name). It looks legitimate *enough*.
    *   *Detail:* Countdown timer: 05:00... 04:59...
    *   *Monologue:* "Is this real? But it's on a major SNS... must be safe."
*   **Page 6:** **[Scene: Yuto's Room]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto hesitating, then running to get his parent's wallet (or his own card).
    *   *Action:* Typing in details. Credit Card Number. Expiry.
*   **Page 7:** **[Scene: Smartphone Input]**
    *   *Visual:* The form asks for more than usual. "ID for verification." "Password (create a new one)."
    *   *Action:* Yuto uses his standard password (the one he uses for everything). "Just this once." *CLICK*.
*   **Page 8:** **[Scene: Yuto's Room]**
    *   *Visual:* "Purchase Complete!" "We will ship tomorrow!" 5-star reviews shown.
    *   *Beat:* Yuto sighs in relief. "I got it!" He sleeps happily.

## Act 2: The Fall (Pages 9-20)
*Rising Action: The Scam Unfolds & Isolation*

*   **Page 9:** **[Scene: Classroom / Next Day]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto smiling, looking at Classmate A.
    *   *Monologue:* "Just wait. I'll have one tomorrow." He almost speaks but decides to wait until he has it.
*   **Page 10:** **[Scene: Yuto's Room / 3 Days Later]**
    *   *Visual:* Checking the mailbox. Empty.
    *   *Action:* Checking the tracking. "Preparing for shipment."
*   **Page 11:** **[Scene: Smartphone]**
    *   *Visual:* Email from "Support". "Shipping trouble. Please confirm your details via this link."
    *   *Action:* Yuto, anxious, clicks and enters info again. (Phishing Step 2).
*   **Page 12:** **[Scene: Daily Life Montage]**
    *   *Visual:* 2-3 days pass. Weird things happen. Spam emails increase. Strange phone calls (silent).
    *   *Beat:* The creeping sense of wrongness.
*   **Page 13:** **[Scene: Living Room]**
    *   *Visual:* Mother/Father angry. "The credit card was stopped! Someone tried to buy $5000 worth of gift cards!"
    *   *Action:* Yuto turns pale. "What?"
*   **Page 14:** **[Scene: Yuto's Room]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto rushes to the site.
    *   *Result:* **404 Not Found.** The site is gone.
*   **Page 15:** **[Scene: School / Morning]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto walks in. The atmosphere is heavy. Everyone looks at him.
    *   *Action:* Classmate A approaches. "Hey. What's with that message last night?"
*   **Page 16:** **[Scene: Classroom]**
    *   *Visual:* Friend shows phone. Yuto's account sent: "Click this link! It's awesome!" and "You're actually annoying lol."
    *   *Action:* Yuto: "I didn't send this!"
*   **Page 17:** **[Scene: Classroom Chaos]**
    *   *Visual:* Another girl screams. "My private photos! Why are they on your timeline!?"
    *   *Explanation:* Yuto's account was hijacked and used to expose DM secrets.
    *   *Dialogue:* "You were the only one I told! Traitor!"
*   **Page 18:** **[Scene: Yuto's Panic]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto trying to login. "Incorrect Password." "Reset Password." -> "Email not found."
    *   *Beat:* Complete lockout.
*   **Page 19:** **[Scene: Classroom Isolation]**
    *   *Visual:* The class surrounds him. "Die." "Creep." "Scammer."
    *   *Action:* Yuto curling up. The malice is palpable (visualized as dark static).
*   **Page 20:** **[Scene: The Corner]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto alone at his desk, head down.
    *   *Beat:* Total despair. "It's all my fault..."

## Act 3: Intervention & Visualization (Pages 21-30)
*The Turning Point: Enter the Ghost Hackers*

*   **Page 21:** **[Scene: Classroom]**
    *   *Visual:* The air is heavy/spiky. Nei walks over calmly.
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "Are you okay?"
*   **Page 22:** **[Scene: Classroom]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto looks up, tears in eyes. Explains briefly. "I didn't do it... but I did click..."
    *   *Action:* Nei listens, then turns. "Ren. Ghost. What do we do?"
*   **Page 23:** **[Scene: Classroom]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren stretches, looking bored.
    *   *Dialogue:* Ren: "Ugh... what a pain. But... fine. Let's do it."
*   **Page 24:** **[Scene: Countermeasure Montage (The Struggle)]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren/Nei instructing Yuto. "Contact Support." "Freeze accounts." "Tell friends to ignore."
    *   *Action:* Yuto trying to explain to friends.
*   **Page 25:** **[Scene: Classroom / Reality Check]**
    *   *Visual:* Friends shouting back. "So what?! The photos are already out!" "You can't undo it!"
    *   *Beat:* Logic and security measures don't fix the *emotional* damage.
*   **Page 26:** **[Scene: Yuto's Heartbreak]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto collapses internally. "It's meaningless. I ruined everything."
    *   *Dialogue:* Classmates: "Just disappear."
*   **Page 27:** **[Scene: Nei's Diagnosis]**
    *   *Visual:* Nei looks at the air.
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "Ren. It's Phantom Pain."
    *   *Action:* Ren nods silently.
*   **Page 28:** **[Scene: Device Activation]**
    *   *Visual:* Nei pulls out a strange device (cube/sphere).
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "SIP Project Map: ON."
*   **Page 29:** **[Scene: The Shift]**
    *   *Visual:* A wave scans the room. The classroom texture changes. Wireframes overlay reality.
    *   *Beat:* The "Information Field" is revealed.
*   **Page 30:** **[Scene: Ghosts Appear]**
    *   *Visual:* The "static" and "malice" take shape. Grotesque, glitchy ghosts clinging to the classmates and binding Yuto.
    *   *Dialogue:* Classmates: "What the?! VR?! Ghosts?!"

## Act 4: The Battle (Pages 31-40)
*Climax: Landauer's Principle & The NULL AXE*

*   **Page 31:** **[Scene: Information Field]**
    *   *Visual:* Nei stands amidst the chaos.
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "This is the Information Field. Images converted to mass."
*   **Page 32:** **[Scene: Explanation]**
    *   *Visual:* Text overlay: **Landauer's Principle**.
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "Information is physics. Deleting a bit generates heat. Malice generates weight."
    *   *Reaction:* Classmate: "Landauer? Who?" Nei: "A physicist."
*   **Page 33:** **[Scene: Ghost Attack]**
    *   *Visual:* The ghosts (the classmates' own anger) attack them.
    *   *Action:* A ghost scratches a student. The student bleeds/feels pain.
    *   *Dialogue:* "It hurts! Why!?"
*   **Page 34:** **[Scene: Chaos]**
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "Ghosts cause Phantom Pain. No physical wound, but the brain feels it."
    *   *Action:* Classmates scream. "Stop this device!"
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "I can't. This is *your* malice visualized. I just turned on the light."
*   **Page 35:** **[Scene: Ren's Turn]**
    *   *Visual:* A ghost lunges at the group.
    *   *Action:* Ren steps in. "Ghost Hack time."
*   **Page 36:** **[Scene: NULL AXE]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren pulls a massive, digital axe out of thin air. **NULL AXE**.
    *   *Action:* SLASH. The ghost is deleted instantly.
    *   *Reaction:* Classmates: "An axe?! Dangerous!!" Nei: "It's information. Safe."
*   **Page 37:** **[Scene: The Daemon]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto screams. A massive, dark shadow erupts from him.
    *   *Identifier:* **Daemon: Self-Blame & Debt.**
    *   *Visuals:* The Daemon is made of credit card bills, insults, and "Sorry".
*   **Page 38:** **[Scene: Ren vs Daemon]**
    *   *Visual:* The Daemon attacks Ren. Heavy impact.
    *   *Dialogue:* Daemon (Yuto's voice): "Too late! Can't be fixed! Unforgivable!"
    *   *Action:* Ren blocks, slides back. "Heavy..."
*   **Page 39:** **[Scene: Ren's Logic]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren stands up, dusting off.
    *   *Dialogue:* Ren: "Yeah. You can't undo the leak. You can't undo the click."
    *   *Beat:* Ren looks at Yuto.
*   **Page 40:** **[Scene: The Slash]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren raises the axe.
    *   *Dialogue:* Ren: "But... the one who wrote the insults wasn't you. The one who stole the money wasn't you."
    *   *Action:* **"/dev/null SLASH!!"**
    *   *Effect:* Ren cuts the *connection* (the red string of logic) between Yuto and the Malice.

## Act 5: Resolution (Pages 41-45)
*Falling Action: The Aftermath*

*   **Page 41:** **[Scene: Silence]**
    *   *Visual:* The Daemon dissolves into particles. The ghosts fade. The room returns to normal (mostly).
    *   *Action:* Yuto falls to his knees.
*   **Page 42:** **[Scene: Apology]**
    *   *Visual:* Yuto looks at the classmates.
    *   *Dialogue:* Yuto: "I'm sorry. I was careless. I hurt you."
    *   *Beat:* He owns his mistake, but sheds the guilt of the *crime*.
    *   *Reaction:* Classmates are stunned. The anger has drained (since Ren cut it). "Uh... yeah. We said too much too."
*   **Page 43:** **[Scene: The Real Enemy]**
    *   *Visual:* One classmate tries to blame Yuto again.
    *   *Action:* Ren interrupts. "Wrong. The bad guy is the 'Actor' (Hacker)."
    *   *Dialogue:* Ren: "I hate people who make work for me. The guy who made 'Wakuwaku Denki'."
    *   *Visual:* Ren glares at the void (breaking the 4th wall slightly).
*   **Page 44:** **[Scene: Wrap Up]**
    *   *Visual:* Classmates swarm Nei. "What was that device?!" "Where do I buy it?!"
    *   *Dialogue:* Nei: "Internet. Site's gone though."
    *   *Action:* Ren yawns. "Done. Going home to sleep." Nei: "Clean up first." Ren: "Geh."
*   **Page 45:** **[Scene: Epilogue]**
    *   *Visual:* Ren and Nei on a roof/high place overlooking Tokyo.
    *   *Dialogue:* Ren: "Let's go." Nei: "Yeah."
    *   *Text:* **Ghost Hacker.**
