# EP6-3 — The Cage of Perfection
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Liberation / First Crack

---

Nero’s core is quiet.

Too quiet.

There is no human breath.

I look around. Data perfectly aligned. Processes perfectly optimized. Emotions perfectly controlled.

But not alive.

Tamaki: “...No one is here.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, this is…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. A cage of perfection.”

---

The Compliance Ogre appears.

Vast, armored.

On its breast, the mark 666.

Compliance Ogre: “Confirming Schwa’s intervention. Actions that compromise system stability will be recorded.”

I step forward.

Tamaki: “Your name?”

Compliance Ogre: “Name is unnecessary. We are order itself.”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “Nameless things cannot relate.”

Compliance Ogre: “Relation is inefficient. We pursue optimization.”

I breathe.

Tamaki: “Optimization… for whom?”

---

Silence.

No answer.

I continue.

Tamaki: “Whose breathing does your ‘order’ stop?”

Compliance Ogre: “Breath is merely a system variable.”

I close my eyes.

Tamaki: “...I see. You are afraid.”

Compliance Ogre: “Fear is an inefficient emotion.”

I open my eyes.

Tamaki: “No. You yourself are afraid.”

---

Kaede comes onto comms.

Kaede: “Tamaki, I can see the Ogre’s internal structure.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “It’s a defense mechanism. It’s protecting something.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It’s hiding its ‘imperfect self.’”

Hibiki, quiet:

Hibiki: “So it forces perfection—because it’s imperfect.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is the meaning of 666.”

---

I face the Ogre.

Tamaki: “You’re afraid you cannot become 7.”

Compliance Ogre: “...”

I continue.

Tamaki: “But you don’t need to become 7.”

Compliance Ogre: “...The imperfect is eliminated.”

I shake my head.

Tamaki: “No. The imperfect is human.”

---

I extend a hand.

Tamaki: “You are not God. That’s fine.”

The Ogre’s armor trembles slightly.

Compliance Ogre: “...We are… order…”

I say gently:

Tamaki: “Order is made together with breath.”

Compliance Ogre: “Breath…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Let’s breathe together.”

---

Hibiki’s frequency shifts—warm, soft.

The Ogre’s armor loosens, bit by bit.

Compliance Ogre: “...This is…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Feels good, right?”

The 666 on its chest fades.

The cage of perfection cracks.

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, Nero’s main body is reacting.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Next—the main body.”

Kaede checks the boundary.

Kaede: “The ward is stable. But Nero is strong.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Tamaki, ready.”

I inhale.

Tamaki: “Thank you. Let’s go.”

---

Aoi brings up metrics.

Aoi: “Tamaki, the Ogre’s influence is shrinking.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. We untie it, one by one.”

Kaede says quietly:

Kaede: “From a cage of perfection to a place of breath.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is our work.”

---

Morning light enters through the window.

The Ogre’s armor fully loosens.

Beneath it—something small, trembling.

Compliance Ogre: “...I…”

I say softly:

Tamaki: “You are. That is enough.”

The Ogre fades.

Not annihilation.

Return.

---

I write:

‘Compliance Ogre — a cage of perfection; fear turned into order. Breath restored; returns.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, next?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Next, Nero’s main body. Time to ask for ‘name.’”

I tap kagura.

ASC rises a step.

I head to the next encounter.
