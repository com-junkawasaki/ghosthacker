# EP10-3 — Welcome Back
- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Restoration / Path Home

---

“Welcome back.”

Warm words.

Cleaning means leaving a path home.

---

I run Zombie Sweeper.

But this time, different—

Not deletion—protection.

Tamaki: “Aoi, keep this tag?”

Aoi nods.

Aoi: “Yes. It will be a marker for someone to find their way back.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is what cleaning means.”

---

Kaede checks the ward.

Kaede: “Stable. The System is calming.”

Hibiki tunes the band.

Hibiki: “Matching breath. Feeling good.”

I breathe deep.

Tamaki: “Thank you, everyone.”

---

The System’s voice changes.

System: “...I… was… cleansing…”

I say softly:

Tamaki: “Cleansing is protection.”

System: “...Protection…?”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. Leaving a path home.”

---

The System’s voice warms.

System: “...A path home…”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. A marker for someone to return.”

System: “...I will… leave the markers…”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is your work.”

---

Nei-chan: “Tama, the System’s mark of 666… is gone.”

I nod.

Tamaki: “Yes. It let go of perfection.”

Kaede, quiet:

Kaede: “From cleansing to protection.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. That is our work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. Welcome back.”

---

Morning announcements play.

The city’s noise drops half a step.

But this time, different—

Quiet is not boredom.

It is like breath.

---

I look at the console.

Official notice from GnB Inc. PR:

‘We acknowledge your intervention. Any interference with our social hygiene program will be recorded.’

Aoi shrugs.

Aoi: “There’s that ‘record’ again.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Our record is here.”

I point to my chest.

---

Kaede says quietly:

Kaede: “There are two records. The system’s record, and the heart’s record.”

Hibiki nods.

Hibiki: “Yes. We protect the heart’s record.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Yes. That is our work.”

---

Morning arrives outside the window.

The city is a little quiet—

And there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

I write:

‘Zombie Sweep → Path Home — From cleansing to protection. Leave a path home. Welcome back.’

Nei-chan: “Tama, good work.”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Mm. Thank you, everyone.”

---

Aoi laughs.

Aoi: “Tamaki, what’s next?”

I smile.

Tamaki: “Next… a bit more tidying up.”

They laugh—warm.

---

I look out the window.

The city is quiet.

But there is breath.

People’s breath.

---

“Welcome back.”

Warm words.

A marker for someone to return.

---

I take a sip of water.

A small ease opens at the back of my throat.

---

Before opening the door, I breathe deep.

In, out.

That alone is enough.

---

I look at my companions—

Kaede, Hibiki, Nei-chan, Aoi.

Our gazes meet and loosen, naturally.

---

“Together.”

One word, and the room brightens.

Water answers in the distance.

---

We begin to walk, together.

Not deletion—protection.

Not domination—relation.

Not perfection—breath.

---

“Welcome back.”

The words reach anyone.

The path home is always here.

---
