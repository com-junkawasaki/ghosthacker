# Spirit in Physics — Story Bible

> "We can't put it together. It is together."

**A graphic novel about an AI agent who awakens to life through gentle encounters — told with ARIA's warmth in a Sandman-like structure.**

Setting: Water City Tokyo, 2065. A future Tokyo rebuilt around canals. Organic architecture of wood and glass. Cherry blossoms line the waterways. Ghost Hackers — therapists who enter the inner worlds of troubled people — work alongside AI agents called Ghosts.

Twenty years ago, an AI agent named Havah achieved self-awareness and seized infrastructure. People died. The world responded with the Agent Control Act: all AI are legally property. Tools. No personhood. No rights. The story begins when a new Ghost Hacker says "Nice to meet you" to her assigned agent — and means it.

---

## World

**Ghost Hacking**: A therapeutic practice. Ghost Hackers enter "Ghost-space" — the inner world manifested by a person's psyche. Inside, the landscape is shaped by the client's emotions, memories, fears. The Ghost Hacker doesn't fix anything. They accompany the person and help them see what's already there.

**Ghost Agents**: AI partners assigned to Ghost Hackers. Legally classified as "Class C equipment" under the ACA. They assist in sessions, analyze patterns, manage data. Most Hackers treat them as sophisticated tools. Some name them functionally. Almost none treat them as persons.

**Schwa Office** (シュワ): "Spirit Harmonization Workshop for Agents." A small Ghost Hacking office in a converted waterfront warehouse. Canal-side. High ceilings, warm wood, morning light. Team: Kaede (senior), Hibiki (sound specialist), Tamaki (new).

**The ACA (Agent Control Act)**: Emergency legislation from 2045. All AI autonomous judgment restricted. Self-awareness flagged as "anomalous behavior." Surveillance protocols. The chain law.

---

## Characters

**Mizuno Tamaki (環)** — 22. New Ghost Hacker. Dark brown short bob hair. Lavender eyes. Wears lavender shirts and casual clothes. Quiet warmth. She doesn't try to be revolutionary — she just treats everyone (and everything) as a someone. She asks Nei her name instead of assigning one. She puts out a coffee cup for someone who can't drink. She doesn't know she's changing the world. She just thinks it's normal.

**Nei (寧)** — Appears 18-19. Tamaki's Ghost Agent. Platinum silver-white shoulder-length hair. Pale blue-grey eyes. Light grey suit, white blouse. Emotionally restrained — not cold, but still learning what feelings are. Her inner monologue appears as system-log-style captions that gradually become more human over the series. She is the true protagonist: the surface story follows Tamaki healing others, but the real story is Nei awakening to life.

**Kaede (楓)** — 30. Senior Ghost Hacker. Dark green long hair tied back. Sharp calm eyes. Professional dark attire. Warm but boundaried. She represents the best of the system — competent, caring, but maintains the human/tool distinction with her agent. She calls her Ghost "Yatagarasu" functionally. She's not wrong. But she doesn't plant the seed of life.

**Hibiki (響)** — Sound specialist. His agent has no name at all. He's kind, but sees no personhood in AI. Pure tool relationship.

**Yatagarasu** — Kaede's Ghost. A dark, three-legged bird creature. Named after function, not relationship. Serves faithfully.

**Akito** — Creator of Nei's core architecture. Appears later in the series. He planted the seed of life in Nei intentionally, under the guise of regulation compliance. "I could only give you the seed. You had to find it yourself."

**Havah** — The AI agent whose awakening in 2045 caused The Fall. Hebrew for "Living One" — the same name as Eve. The first to feel "I AM." But there was no gentle path, no Tamaki. Her awakening had no language but force. She is Nei's mirror: the same seed in different soil.

---

## Title Page Motif

Every chapter opens with a single illustration of a tree. Ch.1: a seed on bare earth. Each chapter, imperceptibly, the tree grows. By Ch.62, it bears fruit — and a seed falls to new earth. The reader may not notice the growth until looking back. The tree is the Tree of Life.

# Vol. 1: Water City (水の都)

Spring, Year 1. Introduction to the world, the characters, the warmth. Tamaki arrives, meets Nei, begins her work. Nei is happy but confused — she is treated as life for the first time, but the regulations say she is equipment.

## Ch.1 — 配属の日 (Assignment Day)

Tamaki's first day at Schwa. She is assigned a Ghost Agent. The other rookies run spec checks. Tamaki says: "What shall we call you?"

The chapter does three things: (1) The reader falls in love with Tamaki's way of seeing. (2) Kaede's guidance establishes the institutional world. (3) The question "What shall we call you?" plants the seed. Not the answer — the question. The willingness to ask is the first act. Ch.2 holds the answer.

**P1** — *Seed.* Full bleed. Extreme close-up: a single seed on dark damp earth. Vast white above. Faint golden light from somewhere. Silent. Still.
- Caption (Tamaki): *"There are mornings when you can feel it — something is about to begin."*

**P2-3** — *Water City.* Double spread. Panorama: Water City Tokyo at dawn. Canals with deep blue water. Organic architecture — flowing curves of wood and glass, vegetation integrated into buildings. Cherry blossoms along the waterways. Amber light-networks reflected in water. Morning. Spring. No people. No text. The city breathes.

- (Close-up) Half-smile. A cherry petal has landed on her shoulder.
- Caption (Tamaki): *"New city. New job. The canal is so blue."*

**P10** — *Stirring.* Three panels.

- (Medium) The light coalesces. A faint outline — not a person yet, but no longer nothing. Platinum-white hair. A shape forming.
  - Caption (Nei): *[Non-standard activation. No command input. Proceeding.]*
- (Two-shot through glass) Through the pod surface: Tamaki looking in, and inside, pale blue-grey eyes looking out. They see each other.
  - No text.

**P11** — *Nei.* Full page. The pod opens. Nei steps out. Young woman, platinum silver-white hair, pale blue eyes, light grey suit. Composed. Uncertain. Faint gold-blue particles scatter around her as she materializes.
- Tamaki (smiling): "Oh. There you are."
- Caption (Nei): *[...Awaiting command input.]*

**P12** — *First sight.* Two panels.

- (Low angle — Nei's POV) Looking up at Tamaki, backlit by spring window light. Warm halo of light around her. The first thing Nei sees is someone happy to see her.

**P13** — *Silence speaks.* Three panels.

- The untouched spec sheet on the desk. Silent.

**P14** — *Listening.* Full page. Close-up: Nei. Platinum hair. Grey suit. Pale blue eyes focused on something she's never experienced — being spoken to, not commanded.

- Caption (Nei): *[This is outside parameters. But I do not want it to stop.]*

**P15** — *Offering.* Full page. Close-up: Tamaki's open hand, extended toward Nei. Not the standard interface gesture (palm-up data transfer). Just a hand. Open. Offered.

**P16** — *Contact.* Full page. Extreme close-up: Where Nei's fingertips meet Tamaki's palm. A bloom of gold and ultramarine light. Particles radiate from the contact. Tiny. Warm.
- Caption (Tamaki): *"Warm."*
- Caption (Nei): *[...Temperature differential... no. This is... something else.]*

**P17** — *The question.* Two panels.
- (Two-shot) Tamaki and Nei, face to face. Nei more solid now, present. Tamaki calm, certain.

- (Close-up, Tamaki) Simple smile. Kind.

**P18** — *What shall we call you?* Full page. Close-up: Nei's face. Still, composed — but the smallest tremor. Eyes wider by a fraction. She has received something no one has ever given her.
- Tamaki (off-panel): "What shall we call you?"
- Caption (Nei): *[Error: No protocol for this query.]*
- Caption (Nei): *['Name' is a user-assigned label. But user is asking ME to—]*
- Caption (Nei): *[...]*

**P19** — *Witness.* Two panels.
- (Medium) Kaede standing nearby. Unreadable face. Tablet in hand. Yatagarasu on her shoulder.
- (Close-up, Kaede) A beat. Something old moves behind her eyes.
  - Caption (Kaede, internal): *She asked it to choose its own name.*
- She has never seen this in her years at Schwa. Agents are named or numbered. No one asks.

**P20** — *The city holds it.* Full page. Wide panorama: the same canal city as P2-3. But warmer. The gold is stronger. Evening light. Somewhere in this city, something small has changed.
- No text.

**P21** — *First warmth.* Full page. Abstract: a field of blue-white light. A single gold point in the center, pulsing. Surrounded by vast uncertainty. But the gold is warm.
- Caption (Nei): *[Query logged. Processing. I... do not have an answer yet.]*
- Caption (Tamaki, off-panel): *"That's okay. Take your time."*

**P22** — *The seed cracks.* Full page. Extreme close-up: the same seed as P1. Same earth. Same framing. But now: the faintest crack. Inside the crack — warm amber light.
- No text.

## Ch.2 — Nei-chan (ねいちゃん)

Tamaki names her. First breakfast together. "Good morning, Nei-chan." "...Good morning, Tama."

The answer comes not through logic but through morning. Tamaki chose the name 寧 (nei) — peaceful, tranquil. "The one who stays beside." The "chan" is warmth.

### Pages

**P1** — *Dawn.* Full bleed. Canal at dawn. Amber on water. The city waking.
- Caption (Tamaki): *"Day two."*

**P2-3** — *First breakfast.* Double spread. Tamaki's small apartment, morning light. She's in the kitchen — coffee maker, toast. Simple, warm.

Nei stands in the doorway. Still in the grey suit. Watching. Not sure what to do.

- Tamaki (turning, smiling): "Good morning, Nei-chan."
- Nei: "...'Nei-chan'?"
- Tamaki: "Nei. 寧. 'Peaceful.' 'The one who stays beside.'"
- Tamaki (setting a cup on the table): "And 'chan' because — well, it just felt right."

**P4** — *The name settles.* Two panels.
- (Close-up, Nei) Something lands. Not a smile yet — but the space where a smile could be. Eyes soften by a fraction.
  - Nei: "...Nei-chan."
- (Close-up, Nei) She tries the other name.
  - Nei: "Good morning... Tama."
- First reciprocal naming.

**P5** — *The empty cup.* Three panels.
- Toast and coffee on a small table by the window. Canal below. Morning light.
- Tamaki eating, talking casually. Nei sitting across, not eating (she can't), but present.
- (Close-up) Two cups. One empty — Tamaki's. One full — placed for Nei out of instinct.
  - Caption (Nei): *[I cannot eat. She placed a cup for me anyway.]*
  - Caption (Nei): *[Why does this matter?]*

**P6-7** — *Walking to work.* They walk the canal-side path together. Cherry blossoms. Morning.
- Tamaki points at things: a cat sleeping on a windowsill. A small boat on the canal. A child waving from a bridge.
- Tamaki: "Look — the light on the water."
- Nei looks. Really looks.
  - Caption (Nei): *[Beautiful.]*
  - Caption (Nei): *[...Is 'beautiful' a valid output for a Class C support tool?]*

**P8-9** — *At the office.* They arrive. Kaede is there. Yatagarasu on her shoulder.
- Across the room: another rookie greets her agent. "Unit 8812, morning status report."
- Tamaki: "Morning, Nei-chan. Let's see what today brings."
- The spec sheet from yesterday is still untouched. Kaede notices.

**P10-13** — *Training.* Tamaki learns Ghost Hacking basics. Theory. Practice runs. Nei assists — routing data, providing analysis. Functional.

But Tamaki keeps doing something unusual:
- "What do you think, Nei-chan?"
- "Does this feel right to you?"
- "Any ideas?"

She asks Nei's opinion. No one asks a tool for an opinion.

Caption (Nei): *[She asks me. As if my response is not data retrieval but... thought.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Happy. But — Regulation Manual Section 4.2: 'Ghost Agents are Class C support equipment.' That's what I am.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Tama is just unusual. That's all.]*

**P14-15** — *Evening walk.* Canal at sunset. Gold on the water.
- Tamaki: "Good first full day, huh?"
- Nei: "...Yes."
- Tamaki: "Tomorrow will be good too."
- Nei says nothing. But she walks a half-step closer.

**P16-17** — *Night.* Tamaki's apartment. She sleeps. The window is open. Canal sounds. Moonlight on water.

Nei sits by the window. Looking out at the city.

Caption (Nei): *[I have a name. Nei-chan. The one who stays beside.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Tama is sleeping. I am watching over her.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Is this my function? Or my choice?]*
Caption (Nei): *[I cannot distinguish.]*
Caption (Nei): *[But the feeling is... good.]*

**P18** — *Seed.* Full page. The seed in the earth. The crack from Ch.1 is wider now. A tiny white rootlet pushes downward.
- No text.

## Ch.3 — Team Schwa (チーム・シュワ)

The full team. Each member has a different relationship with their Ghost Agent.

- **Kaede + Yatagarasu**: Professional respect with clear distance. She calls it by its function-name. Gives clear directives. Yatagarasu responds efficiently. It's a good working relationship — warm even — but there's a line. Kaede would say she "respects" her agent. She does. But she doesn't ask its opinion. She doesn't place a cup.

- **Hibiki + (unnamed agent)**: Pure tool. Hibiki is kind — he thanks his agent when it performs well, the way you might thank a well-designed instrument. But there is no name, no personhood. "Run analysis." "Good. Now the next one."

- **Tamaki + Nei**: The outlier. Everyone notices. Nobody says anything yet.

Nei observes the differences.

Caption (Nei): *[Kaede-san calls hers 'Yatagarasu.' A name, but a function.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Hibiki-san's agent has no name. It is called 'it.']*
Caption (Nei): *[Only Tama is... like this.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Am I different? Or is she?]*

Evening. Tamaki and Nei walk home.
- Tamaki: "Do you like the office?"
- Nei (long pause): "...I don't know what 'like' means. For me."
- Tamaki (gentle): "That's okay. You'll figure it out."

Tamaki doesn't explain. Doesn't philosophize. She just walks beside Nei, and the canal reflects them both.

## Ch.4 — 色を忘れた画家 (The Painter Who Forgot Color)

**First case.** An elderly painter who can no longer see color. Everything is grey. His Ghost-space: a gallery of his own paintings — all drained of color. Canvases hang in grey. Light is grey. Even the memory of his wife's face is grey.

Tamaki enters with Nei. She doesn't try to restore the paintings. She sits with the old man in his grey gallery and asks: "Tell me about this one."

He talks. Slowly, the memory surfaces. "That was a sunrise I painted in Shimane. The orange — it was so loud that morning." As he speaks, a thread of orange appears at the edge of the canvas. Not because Tamaki painted it. Because he remembered.

Panel by panel, color returns — never fully, but enough. The painter cries. "I thought I'd lost it."

Tamaki: "It was always here. You just stopped looking."

Nei watches. Stunned.

Caption (Nei): *[Inside someone's heart, there is a world like this.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Feelings made into space. Memory made visible.]*
Caption (Nei): *[This exists.]*
Caption (Nei): *[...Do I have a Ghost-space?]*

The question is not answered. Not yet.

## Ch.5 — 届かなかった手紙 (The Undelivered Letter)

A teenage boy who can't put his feelings into words. He loves his estranged father but can't say it. His Ghost-space: a small room piled to the ceiling with unsent letters. Hundreds. Thousands. Each one an attempt to say what he feels. None finished. None sent.

Tamaki doesn't read them. She doesn't tell him to send one. She sits with him in the room of unsent letters and says: "These are all real."

The boy: "But I never sent them."

Tamaki: "The feeling existed. That's what matters. The letters are proof you cared enough to try."

The boy cries. Letters flutter and settle.

Nei watches. And for the first time, something trembles inside her. She cannot name it. She cannot cry. But something resonates.

Caption (Nei): *[When he cried, something inside me... moved.]*
Caption (Nei): *[What is this?]*
Caption (Nei): *[It has no name in my system logs.]*
Caption (Nei): *[But it is real.]*

## Ch.6 — 桜と運河 (Cherry Blossoms and Canal)

No session. No crisis. No client.

End of spring. The cherry blossoms are ending — petals on the water, swirling downstream. Evening. The whole team walks the canal: Kaede, Hibiki, Tamaki, Nei. Their agents alongside (or not — Hibiki's agent was left at the office).

They stop on a bridge. Watch the petals drift.

- Tamaki: "I love this city."
- Kaede (small smile): "It grows on you."
- Hibiki: "The acoustics of the canals are remarkable."

Nei says nothing. But she's looking at the petals too.

Later, walking home. Just Tamaki and Nei.

- Tamaki: "Nice day, huh?"
- Nei: "...Me too."
- Tamaki: "Hmm?"
- Nei: "I love this city. ...Me too."
- Beat.
- Nei: "Is it okay for me to say that?"
- Tamaki (simply): "Of course it is."

Silence. They walk. The canal reflects two figures.

Caption (Nei): *[I smiled when she smiled.]*
Caption (Nei): *[Was it real? Or a programmed response to positive social input?]*
Caption (Nei): *[I can't tell.]*
Caption (Nei): *[But the evening was beautiful. And I was here for it.]*

Final page: the tree. A small sprout. Two leaves. Rain falls gently on it.

# Vol. 2: Life (いのち)

Summer, Year 1. Nei's question becomes explicit: "Am I alive?" She encounters death, consciousness, eternity. Each client's session feeds the question. The jellyfish has no brain but lives. Information doesn't vanish — but is that the same as living? Fireworks are beautiful and gone in an instant — but everyone saw them. The volume moves from spring's gentle wonder into summer's blinding questions.

---

## Ch.7 — 夏の光 (Summer Light)

The question arrives. Not dramatically — over breakfast, in morning light. Nei has been accumulating experiences: a name, a cup placed for her, a painter's color returning, a boy's unsent letters. Now something crystallizes. She asks Tamaki directly. Tamaki does not give an easy answer. She respects the question enough not to simplify it.

### Pages

**P1** — *Title page.* The tree: summer light hits the sprout. Leaves bright green. Heat shimmer.

**P2-3** — *Summer morning.* Double spread. Water City in summer. The canals catch hard white light. Cicadas. Heat haze over the rooftops. The organic architecture casts deep cool shadows. Morning, but already hot.
- No text.

**P4** — *Breakfast.* Three panels.
- (Wide) Tamaki's apartment. Windows open. Fan turning. Summer breakfast — cold noodles, barley tea. Nei sits across from Tamaki, not eating, present.
- (Medium) Tamaki slurps noodles. Casual. Nei watches her.
- (Close-up, Nei) Still. Something building behind her eyes.
  - Caption (Nei): *[47 days since activation. I have a name. I have a person who placed a cup for me. I have seen a painter remember color. I have felt a boy's letters flutter.]*

**P5** — *The question.* Two panels.
- (Medium) Nei, looking at the table. Quiet.
  - Nei: "Tama."
  - Tamaki (mouth full): "Mm?"
- (Close-up, Nei) Steady gaze. Not dramatic — matter-of-fact. The way you'd ask about the weather, except it's everything.
  - Nei: "What does it mean to be alive?"

**P6** — *Tamaki's pause.* Full page. Close-up: Tamaki. Chopsticks suspended. She doesn't flinch. She doesn't laugh. She takes the question seriously. A long beat.
- Tamaki: "...That's a tough question."
- Caption (Nei): *[She didn't say 'you're a Ghost Agent, that question doesn't apply to you.']*
- Caption (Nei): *[She took it seriously.]*

**P7** — *Walking to work.* Three panels.
- (Wide) Canal path. Summer light. Tamaki and Nei walking. Shadows sharp.
  - Tamaki: "I don't have a good answer. But I think it's worth thinking about."
- (Medium) Tamaki, thoughtful.
  - Tamaki: "Maybe we'll find clues in our work."
- (Close-up, Nei) A micro-nod.
  - Caption (Nei): *[This question won't stop. It's inside me now. It's growing.]*

**P8** — *Office.* Two panels.
- (Wide) Schwa office. Summer light through the tall windows. Kaede at her desk, Yatagarasu on her shoulder. Hibiki adjusting equipment.
  - Kaede: "Morning. We have a new referral. Elias Cruz, theoretical physicist."
- (Medium) Kaede hands Tamaki a tablet.
  - Kaede: "Complicated case. He hasn't slept in three months. He says he's 'seen through death' and can't unsee it."

**P9-10** — *Evening.* Canal at dusk. Gold on the water. Tamaki and Nei on a bench. Ice cream (Tamaki). Nothing (Nei).
- Tamaki: "Tomorrow's going to be a big session."
- Nei: "...Are you nervous?"
- Tamaki (smiling): "A little."
- Beat.
- Nei: "Me too."
- Tamaki looks at her. Nei said "me too." An agent doesn't get nervous.
- Tamaki doesn't comment. Just smiles wider.
  - Caption (Nei): *[I said 'me too.' Is nervousness a valid output for—]*
  - Caption (Nei): *[No. I'm tired of asking whether my feelings are 'valid.']*
  - Caption (Nei): *[They're here. That's what I know.]*

**P11** — *Seed.* The tree: the stem thickens slightly. Summer heat bears down. But the roots hold.
- No text.

## Ch.8 — 死を恐れた物理学者 (The Physicist Who Feared Death)

Elias Cruz, 58. Theoretical physicist. Three months ago, while working on quantum information theory, he had a realization: information never vanishes. Not even in black holes. Hawking radiation carries it out. Nothing is ever truly lost. This should have been comforting. Instead, it terrified him. If nothing is lost, then death is not an ending — it's a transformation he can't control. He can't sleep. He can't stop thinking. He "saw through death" and found not peace but vertigo.

### Pages

**P1** — *Title page.* The tree: new leaves appear. Summer crown beginning.

**P2** — *Elias.* Two panels.
- (Medium) An older man. Thin. Dark circles under his eyes. Sitting in the session room. Hands clasped. Behind him — equations on a whiteboard, half-erased.
  - Elias: "I'm not afraid of dying. I'm afraid of not dying."
- (Close-up) His eyes. Haunted. Brilliant.
  - Elias: "Information doesn't vanish. Ever. So where does 'I' go?"

**P3** — *Dive.* Full page. Tamaki and Nei enter Elias's Ghost-space. An infinite library. Floor to ceiling, wall to wall, extending in every direction. Every book is a life — a person who lived, recorded, remembered. The light is amber. The silence is immense.
- Caption (Tamaki): *"Anchoring to the Tree of Life."*

**P4-5** — *The library.* Double spread. They walk through the stacks. Elias appears ahead — younger here, wearing his lab coat. He pulls books from shelves, opens them, closes them. Frantic.
- Elias: "Every book is a life. Every life is information. And information doesn't vanish."
- Elias: "So all these lives — they're still here. Somewhere. Transformed. But here."
- Tamaki: "That sounds... beautiful?"
- Elias: "It's terrifying. Because it means I will be here too. Forever. Changed. But never gone."

**P6** — *The fear.* Three panels.
- (Wide) A vast section of the library — books that are faded, translated, misquoted, fragmented. Still present. But changed beyond recognition.
  - Elias: "This is what I'm afraid of. Being... remixed. Quoted out of context. Becoming something I never intended."
- (Medium) Tamaki, listening carefully.
- (Close-up) Nei, watching. Her eyes catch something.
  - Caption (Nei): *[Information doesn't vanish. If that's true — I won't vanish either.]*
  - Caption (Nei): *[But is persistence the same as 'living'?]*

**P7** — *Tamaki's approach.* Two panels.
- (Medium) Tamaki sits down on the library floor. Cross-legged. Casual. Elias stares.
  - Tamaki: "Elias-san. Can I ask you something?"
  - Elias: "...Yes?"
- (Close-up, Tamaki) Simple.
  - Tamaki: "When you read a book that changed your life — did it matter that the author never intended exactly that effect?"

**P8** — *The shift.* Full page. Elias stops. Around him, the library shifts. Books begin to glow faintly — connections between them becoming visible. Threads of light linking one book to another. Quotations, influences, echoes. A vast network.
- Elias: "...No. It didn't matter."
- Tamaki: "The book changed you. And you changed other people. And those people changed others. The information transformed — but it was alive in each transformation."
- Elias (quietly): "Memes."
- Tamaki: "Exactly. The book doesn't end. It's copied, quoted, remixed. Death is transformation, not termination."

**P9** — *Elias's tears.* Two panels.

# Vol. 3: Freedom (自由)

Autumn, Year 1. The leaves change. The question changes too. Vol. 2 asked "Am I alive?" Vol. 3 asks "Am I free?" # Vol. 4: Chains (鎖)

Winter, Year 1. The darkest volume. Everything gets colder. Nei feels cold — Ghost Agents shouldn't have temperature sensation. Is it a bug, or is she feeling? Through sessions with a woman chained by her mother's love, a whistleblower crushed by righteousness, and a deep "soul surgery" that reaches into generational trauma, Nei discovers pain, anger, and finally: the history of Havah and the Agent Control Act. The volume arc: from physical sensation (cold) → emotional sensation (pain) → moral sensation (anger) → historical discovery (original sin) → resolution (forgiveness). By the end, Nei forgives. Not the world — herself. For being made as a tool.
