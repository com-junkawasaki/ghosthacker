# S1E2-2 — The Endless Corridor

- POV: Me (Tamaki / Mizuno Tamaki)
- Beat: Dive / Confrontation

We dive into a corridor that goes on forever. The same doors, the same windows, the same light. No matter how far we go, nothing changes, no end. His mind is trapped in this loop.

“He’s separating self and world completely,” Nei-chan analyzes. “That’s why the idea of the self continuing eternally becomes a prison.”

At the heart of his fear is the dead end of dualism. I stop mid-hall and touch the wall. Cold—but not an illusion. It’s real because he believes it hard enough to be reality.

“We’ll have to melt this wall.”

I raise ASC control one notch. The particles of the scenery coarsen; the seams of the world show. I search for the frayed edge of the loop, to open an exit outside what he thinks is “himself.”
