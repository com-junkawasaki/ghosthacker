# Session: Lachlan Keyes - Part 3

---
Aoi (comms): “Outside, the cicadas started. Coastal flood map update came in—okay to hold?”

Kaede: “Hold.”

Quiet, firm.

Kaede: “Focus on him now. We’ll review the map later.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, breathe with him.”

The wall is down, and he shows himself for the first time. He trembles, and he cries.

“...I couldn’t trust anyone. No one… trusted me.”

I take his hand. The cold begins to warm.

“I’m here now. You want to trust—that’s your humanity.”

He smiles unexpectedly, then frowns, unsure. “...This face—it’s been a while.”

Nei-chan: “Trust is ‘not separated.’ Tama and I are like that.”

He smiles. “Illogical… but beautiful.”

Kaede returns, a little relief in her voice: “Your heart was still in orbit. Now it returned. Welcome back, Lachlan.”

I ask: “Who do you want to meet next?”

“...My mother. I haven’t called her in eighteen months.”

“Call her when you’re back. Teach her the ‘feeling good’ breathing.”

Nei-chan: “Your mom will go ‘huh?’ But she’ll be relieved right away.”

He nods.

—A short monologue. Childhood under a father’s command. A silent mother. He grew up in a world where ‘his body was not his.’ The first freedom he will reclaim by breathing is the opening of his ribcage.

The next case is projected on the window.

Leo Kazan & Millia Stuart.

I inhale. “…A couple?”

Kaede’s voice carries a hint of worry: “Yes. They say ‘we love each other,’ while hurting each other. A difficult case.”

Nei-chan: “Is that… love?”

I look at the canal’s light. “We’ll find out.”
