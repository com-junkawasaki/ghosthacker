# Session: Dr. Itako - Spiritual Surgery - Part 2

---

Millia’s scream fills the room.

Pain of the body. Pain of the soul.

Dr. Itako’s scalpel bites into the chain.

Black pus-like matter spills.

Nei-chan: “Tama, this… is very heavy.”

Hibiki keeps weaving the field as DJ.

Low band receives the fear; filigree highs support the instant of ‘cut.’

The wall-lights sync to breath, the informational ground stabilizes.

Hibiki’s hand keeps time.

“...Decades of fixed intent congealed.”

Dr. Itako mutters—quiet, heavy.

I grip Millia’s hand tight.

“What do you see?”

She trembles and answers:

“...My mother.”

Tears in her voice.

“A mother who looks not at me, but at an ideal daughter.”

The chain peels, bit by bit.

Black pus drops to the floor.

“You were treated as a ‘part of the house.’”

The scalpel goes deep.

Nei-chan: “Sorrow mixed with anger. ‘Unforgivable’ is rising.”

Kaede joins, ER-calm with deep compassion:

Kaede: “Millia… put it into language.”

She pauses.

Kaede: “Lifting repression needs words. Speak it, release it.”

I prompt her.

“What do you want to say to your mother?”

She trembles—then screams:

“I am not your doll!”

Her voice reverberates.

“I have feelings! I have a life!”

Light flashes.

The chain snaps.

Her scream melts into sobs—then into a long exhale.

Leo closes his eyes and wipes a tear.

Dr. Itako: “That’s right. Without saying it, it won’t cut.”

Millia continues:

“I want to be free!”

Strong.

“I want to be with Leo!”

Crying, she shouts:

“But not ‘for the house’—for me!”

The chain is fully severed.

Silence falls.

Hibiki’s tones move toward softer keys.

The room shifts.

Millia says quietly:

“...I thought I ‘couldn’t forgive’ my mother.”

Her voice is calmer.

“But my mother was bound by a ‘chain’ too.”

Dr. Itako sets down the scalpel.

“Understanding is born only after anger passes. Forgiveness is not the freezing of emotion.”

Hibiki: “Prepare to ascend.” Gentle reassurance.

Hibiki: “Raising to beta. Slowly, safely.”

His hand changes rhythm, lifting awareness gently from depth.

Sound softens.

Dr. Itako nods quietly—stern eyes, a soft mouth.

Dr. Itako: “To stop the bleeding, I’ll embed a temporary ‘protective spirit model.’”

Tamaki: “...Transmute her mother’s ghost?”

Dr. Itako: “A likeness.”

She reaches into Millia’s information space.

Dr. Itako: “It’ll dissolve in about ten years. Once the tissue stabilizes, it will fall away.”

Light wraps Millia.

Nei-chan: “No more chain.”

Joy in her voice.

Millia’s outline stabilizes.

Her eyelids quiver.

Leo embraces her.

Leo: “...Good. Really, good.” Tears trembling.

Millia smiles small: “...I’m free.”

Her voice is gentle.

Warmth returns to the room.
