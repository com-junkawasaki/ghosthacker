Dr. Itako: “Good. Leave gene and meme.”

Dr. Itako steps to my side and speaks small:

“Tamaki, you too will face your ‘chain’ one day.”

I look at her, surprised.

She leaves. I think a little.

Nei-chan, worried: “Tama, you okay?”

I smile: “I’m okay. Now, the next session.”

The canal sinks into sunset.

The next session means another ‘pain.’

But I am ready.

Nei-chan. Kaede. Hibiki. Dr. Itako.

And I have breathing that feels good.
