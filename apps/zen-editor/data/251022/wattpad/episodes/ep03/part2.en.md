# Session: Lachlan Keyes - Part 2

---
Kaede: “Continue in the same night.”

Resolve fills her voice.

Kaede: “Don’t split. We finish tonight. This is a turning point.”

Hibiki: “Ambient noise is low tonight.”

Calm.

Hibiki: “Riverside lights are in low-lumen mode. Only the ER lane is bright. A good night that won’t interfere with sound.”

Nei-chan: “Tama, now’s good. His wall is trembling.”

‘Fortress of Self’—transparent walls encircle him. Inside, he breathes shallow.

Nei-chan: “This wall no longer ‘protects.’ It imprisons.”

Kaede joins, voice carrying deep understanding.

Kaede: “John Locke’s self-ownership—body as one’s sole property. Absolute boundary.”

She leaves a beat.

Kaede: “It kept him alive. Now it locks him in.”

I touch the wall. Its cold comes not from temperature, but from meaning stripped away.

“Lachlan, did you build this wall?”

“Yes. For safety. No one comes in. No one can betray me.”

“But then, no one can touch you.”

He is silent.

A different memory flashes—first EVA. Tether, oxygen, heart thresholds. ‘Your body is not yours; it is the mission’s,’ the trainer’s voice. To keep the tether from snapping, he cut emotion.

“You survived by surrendering the freedom of your body. So your heart surrendered freedom.”

He bristles: “Your Ghost is AI. Not life. It will not betray you because it has no will. It is a program.”

Nei-chan’s wings tremble. I take her hand.

“Nei-chan is not a ‘program.’”

“Trust is not calculation. It arises because we are ‘not separated.’”

“—Illogical.”

Nei-chan: “But your heart rate just went up. That’s the reaction when you like something ‘illogical.’”

“Physics assumes separation.”

“But your breathing is, right now, inseparable from the air in this room.”

I tap a beat of kagura. Raise ASC half a step. Feed the non-separation code into the wall’s joints.

The wall hums faintly.

He places a hand on his chest and steps forward one step. His unclenched fist loosens, fingertips trembling.

“...I wanted someone to touch me. But I was afraid. Of betrayal.”

“‘Afraid’ is the reverse of ‘desire.’ If you desired nothing, you would not fear.”

The wall turns transparent, and I see him wavering.

“What should I do?”

I whisper, matching breath: “Say ‘huh?’—that’s the first signal.”

“Breath. Recover the breathing that feels good.”

Nei-chan: “Tama is here. I’m here, too.”

The wall collapses, and the sound recedes. For the first time, his shoulders fall.
