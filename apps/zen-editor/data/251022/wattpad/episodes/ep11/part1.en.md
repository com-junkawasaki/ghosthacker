The paperwork is perfect. That’s why it reeks. I read between the lines. The logs Kaede points to reveal where humans were left behind. Fear, borrowed in the name of justice, was tightening the field.

I give him (the ogre) a name: “Your name is the justification of fear.” A name is a contour. With a contour, a path opens. The institution is not the enemy. It merely forgot how to breathe.

Something eases. Perfection steps back a pace; the human steps forward a half. I open my fist and exhale slowly.

I place “justice that can breathe” on my chest. I close the folder and crack the window.

Cold perfection recedes; a soft trust comes in. Quiet becomes the cue.

Aoi: “Enough. Let’s end here for today.”

I nod. Tension loosens in my chest; relief spreads. Small smiles return.

Light at the window is soft. Hope doesn’t raise its voice—and yet it is surely here.

I send the checklist for Quiet Justice. The institution is not the enemy. It only needs a cue to remember to breathe when it forgets.
